About

About Suede AI.

Suede AI is an agentic platform company founded in 2024 by engineer Jason Colapietro in West Palm Beach, Florida. It builds and runs AI agent platforms that businesses use to manage their work, and white-labels them for agencies and their clients.

What we build.

Suede AI builds and runs AI agent platforms that businesses use to manage their work, and white-labels them for agencies and their clients. What Suede AI does, in order:

  1. Agent platforms for your business: Suede AI Agent Studio. Build agents for the work that repeats, watch every run, and publish an agent as a paid endpoint.
  2. White-label agent platforms: Suede builds and runs an agent platform under an agency’s brand for its clients. Scoped per engagement.
  3. Managed AI agents and AI operations: Suede builds, runs and tunes agents for a business, with every run visible in Agent Studio.
  4. Forward-deployed engineering and a fractional Chief AI Officer, founder-led.
  5. AI search visibility: Suede AI SEO, the GEO, AEO and SEO practice.
  6. Music and creator software: a separate product line.

Company.

Suede AI was founded in 2024 by Jason Colapietro in West Palm Beach, Florida; earlier materials use the name Suede Labs. Its holding company is JC Investment Group LLC, organized in Florida in 2019. The company and its agent platforms live at suedeai.ai. This site, suedeai.org, is Suede AI’s research and investor site: the founder’s record, the investor brief, the books, and research on creator ownership and provenance.

Suede AI’s official sites are suedeai.ai, suedeai.org and their subdomains. It was founded in 2024 by Jason Colapietro.

Founder

Jason Colapietro is an engineer, author, and the founder and CEO of Suede AI. He has 54 pull requests merged across 46 open-source repositories he does not maintain, including Jest, Adobe React Spectrum, Backstage and the Polkadot SDK; he has written five books on Amazon Kindle, including The Claude Code Bible and Codex in Production; and he has shipped 9 iOS apps under his own Apple developer account. He publishes under the pen name Johnny Suede. Five USPTO provisional patent applications, patent pending: 63/946,189, 63/946,192, 63/946,212, 63/946,217 and 63/947,120. Books: The Claude Code Bible, Codex in Production, The Human Authenticity Layer, Proof as Infrastructure and Stake Your Claim on Amazon Kindle, plus The Screenshot (free at seo.suedeai.ai) and The Signal Chain (guitar.solutions).
