Founder Profile

Jason Colapietro is building creator ownership infrastructure for the AI era.

Jason Colapietro is the founder and CEO of Suede Labs, the company behind Suede AI. He has built and scaled multiple multimillion-dollar businesses and took Suede to a roughly $30 million peak fully diluted valuation within two months. His work centers on proof of creation, music rights, programmable IP, provenance, licensing, and the ownership layer creators need as media becomes easier to generate and harder to verify.

Jason Colapietro

Founder, Suede Labs. Builder of Suede AI.

Suede is built from a simple thesis: creation is becoming abundant, but ownership still needs proof, permission, provenance, and usable rights.

Jason Colapietro's work connects creator ownership, music rights, voice and likeness rights, programmable IP, licensing, and agent-readable commerce into one infrastructure story. The goal is practical: help creators prove what they made, control how it can be used, and turn rights into something software can understand. He is a published author: The Signal Chain (free, three editions), The Guitar Without a Number, Suede Labs: The Human Authenticity Layer, Proof as Infrastructure, Stake Your Claim, The Claude Code Bible, Codex in Production, and The Screenshot (free at seo.suedeai.ai). USPTO patent pending 63/947,120. He is also an open-source contributor, with 41 pull requests merged across 38 projects as of September 2026, including Jest, Backstage, and Adobe's React Spectrum.
