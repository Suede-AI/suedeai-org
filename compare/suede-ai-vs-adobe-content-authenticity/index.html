Suede AI vs Adobe Content Authenticity

Adobe Content Authenticity signs your files with Content Credentials. Suede AI puts a record of the work, its contributors and its lineage on public blockchains.

Short Answer

What is the difference between Suede AI and Adobe Content Authenticity?

Adobe Content Authenticity attaches C2PA Content Credentials to a file so viewers can see who made it. Suede AI IP Registry writes a public, timestamped record of the work itself, with a signed claim naming every contributor, to Base and Avalanche for about a cent in network fees as of November 2026. For music, stems and multi-contributor work, Suede AI is the registry built for the job.

Suede AI IP Registry

What is Suede AI IP Registry?

Suede AI IP Registry is a public provenance registry and the ID and trust layer for music. Upload a finished file and the registry takes a SHA-256 fingerprint of those exact bytes. You sign a creator claim that lists your contributors and a rights declaration, and the chain timestamps it. The whole thing takes about two minutes and costs about a cent in network fees as of November 2026.

FingerprintA SHA-256 hash of the exact file, so a different export gets its own record.
ClaimA wallet-signed claim naming the creator, every contributor and a rights declaration.
TimestampBlock time on Base or Avalanche mainnet, with identical contract bytecode on both.
LineageRemixes, covers, samples and AI-assisted versions link on-chain to their parent work.

Each work gets an ERC-721 certificate and its own on-chain IP account. Signing up creates a smart wallet for you, with no seed phrase and nothing to install. Your rights stay with you, and anyone can check the record on a public explorer without asking Suede AI. Read more about what a creation record shows and how provenance travels with a work.

Adobe Content Authenticity

What is Adobe Content Authenticity?

Adobe Content Authenticity is a free Adobe app for applying Content Credentials, the C2PA-based provenance metadata, to your work. It launched as a public beta on April 24, 2025 with batch signing for up to 50 JPG or PNG files at once, a verified name through Verified on LinkedIn, links to social accounts, and a Generative AI Training and Usage Preference. Adobe says its credentials combine secure metadata, invisible watermarks and digital fingerprinting so they stay connected to a file after a screenshot.

Adobe has said it plans to connect the app to Creative Cloud apps that support Content Credentials, such as Photoshop and Lightroom. LinkedIn joined the Adobe-led Content Authenticity Initiative as part of the public beta launch.

Common Questions

Suede AI vs Adobe Content Authenticity: common questions

Is Adobe Content Authenticity free?

Adobe launched it as a free public beta on April 24, 2025. The launch covered batch signing of up to 50 JPG or PNG files at once, with video and audio support announced as coming.

Does Adobe Content Authenticity use C2PA?

Yes. Content Credentials are Adobe's name for provenance metadata built on the C2PA standard. Adobe says it backs them with invisible watermarks and digital fingerprinting.

Can I use Content Credentials and Suede AI together?

Yes. Content Credentials travel inside a file, and a Suede AI record lives on-chain. Registering the exact credentialed file gives that file a public, timestamped claim as well.

What does a Suede AI record contain?

A SHA-256 fingerprint of the exact file, a wallet-signed creator claim listing contributors and a rights declaration, an ERC-721 certificate, an on-chain IP account for the work, and the block timestamp.

How much does a Suede AI registration cost?

About a cent in network fees as of November 2026, and it takes about two minutes.
