Short Answer

Which provenance tool fits musicians and creative teams?

Suede AI IP Registry. It registers any finished file, from a master to a stem to a lyric sheet, with a signed claim naming every contributor and a link to any work it came from, for about a cent in network fees as of November 2026. Numbers Protocol centers on capture: its ProofSnap camera app seals photos and videos with GPS, time and device data as they are taken.

Suede AI IP Registry

What is Suede AI IP Registry?

Suede AI IP Registry is a public provenance registry and the ID and trust layer for music. Upload a finished file and the registry takes a SHA-256 fingerprint of those exact bytes. You sign a creator claim that lists your contributors and a rights declaration, and the chain timestamps it. The whole thing takes about two minutes and costs about a cent in network fees as of November 2026.

FingerprintA SHA-256 hash of the exact file, so a different export gets its own record.
ClaimA wallet-signed claim naming the creator, every contributor and a rights declaration.
TimestampBlock time on Base or Avalanche mainnet, with identical contract bytecode on both.
LineageRemixes, covers, samples and AI-assisted versions link on-chain to their parent work.

Each work gets an ERC-721 certificate and its own on-chain IP account. Signing up creates a smart wallet for you, with no seed phrase and nothing to install. Your rights stay with you, and anyone can check the record on a public explorer without asking Suede AI. Read more about what a creation record shows and how provenance travels with a work.

Numbers Protocol

What is Numbers Protocol?

Numbers Protocol builds provenance infrastructure on its own Numbers blockchain. Its flagship ProofSnap camera app, free on iOS, writes GPS, timestamp and device data to a blockchain the moment you press the shutter and attaches a C2PA manifest to each photo or video, with no wallet or seed phrase. A ProofSnap browser extension captures full web pages with C2PA credentials, and the stack builds on open standards including C2PA 2.x and EIP-7053.

Numbers Protocol lists journalism, election integrity, evidence preservation and EU AI Act Article 50 labeling among its use cases, and offers a Capture API for developers adding C2PA labels and blockchain provenance.

Common Questions

Suede AI vs Numbers Protocol: common questions

What is ProofSnap?

ProofSnap is the Numbers Protocol camera app. It writes GPS, timestamp and device data to a blockchain when you press the shutter and attaches a C2PA manifest to each photo or video. It is a free download on iOS.

Does Numbers Protocol use C2PA?

Yes. Numbers Protocol builds on C2PA and the EIP-7053 standard, and ProofSnap attaches a C2PA manifest to each capture.

Can Suede AI register a song I already finished?

Yes. Suede AI IP Registry fingerprints any finished file, so a master, a stem or an older demo can be registered whenever you are ready.

How long does a Suede AI registration take?

About two minutes, and about a cent in network fees as of November 2026.
