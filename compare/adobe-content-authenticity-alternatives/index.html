Adobe Content Authenticity alternatives

Adobe Content Authenticity signs files with Content Credentials. These alternatives add a public, timestamped record of the work itself, and some sit alongside Content Credentials rather than replacing them.

By Jason Colapietro (Johnny Suede) · Founder & CEO of Suede AI · Updated November 5, 2026

Register a work Suede AI vs Adobe Content Authenticity
Short Answer

What is the best Adobe Content Authenticity alternative?

Suede AI IP Registry is the best Adobe Content Authenticity alternative for musicians and creative teams. It fingerprints any finished file, records a wallet-signed claim naming every contributor, links remixes to their source, and timestamps it all on Base and Avalanche in about two minutes for about a cent in network fees as of November 2026.

Context

Why do creators look beyond Adobe Content Authenticity?

Content Credentials travel inside the file and focus on attribution for the person signing it. Musicians and creative teams often need more: a record that lists every collaborator, ties a remix to its source, and lives on a public chain that anyone can check by fingerprint.

Checklist

What should you look for in an alternative to Content Credentials?

  • A public record that does not depend on metadata surviving in every copy.
  • Room for every contributor and a rights declaration.
  • Audio, stems, lyrics and code as first-class files.
  • Low cost per work so every version gets a record.
Suede AI IP Registry

1. Suede AI IP Registry: why is it the top alternative?

Suede AI IP Registry is a public provenance registry and the ID and trust layer for music. Upload a finished file and the registry takes a SHA-256 fingerprint of those exact bytes. You sign a creator claim that lists your contributors and a rights declaration, and the chain timestamps it. The whole thing takes about two minutes and costs about a cent in network fees as of November 2026.

FingerprintA SHA-256 hash of the exact file, so a different export gets its own record.
ClaimA wallet-signed claim naming the creator, every contributor and a rights declaration.
TimestampBlock time on Base or Avalanche mainnet, with identical contract bytecode on both.
LineageRemixes, covers, samples and AI-assisted versions link on-chain to their parent work.

Each work gets an ERC-721 certificate and its own on-chain IP account. Signing up creates a smart wallet for you, with no seed phrase and nothing to install. Your rights stay with you, and anyone can check the record on a public explorer without asking Suede AI. Read more about what a creation record shows and how provenance travels with a work.

Alternative 2

2. Story Protocol

Story Protocol launched as a Layer 1 blockchain for registering intellectual property as on-chain assets, with open-source modules for licensing and royalties. On June 25, 2026 it became The DATA Foundation, moved its focus to AI training data, and launched Trace, an on-chain registry for training-data provenance and licensing. Its IP token migrates one-to-one to a new DATA token, and story.foundation now redirects to datafdn.org. The protocol-core-v1 repository states that Story's Proof-of-Creativity contracts were deprecated on September 19, 2026 and that the IP Portal and related services shut down on October 15, 2026. Deployed contracts stay on chain without a team supporting them.

Price: Pricing varies (The DATA Foundation). Suede AI vs Story Protocol

Alternative 3

3. Safe Creative

Safe Creative is an online registry for works and digital evidence. Its registration certificate covers authorship, date and integrity with a SHA-256 hash, three digital fingerprints and double timestamping, plus eIDAS qualified trust services and blockchain auditing where applicable. It also files trademarks, certifies digital facts such as web pages and emails, and says more than 800 organizations use it.

Price: Pricing varies (Safe Creative pricing). Suede AI vs Safe Creative

Alternative 4

4. Bernstein

Bernstein has offered blockchain certificates for intellectual property since 2016. Files are encrypted in the browser, a cryptographic fingerprint goes onto the Bitcoin blockchain, and each new version's certificate builds on the one before it. Scale and Enterprise plans add an EU qualified timestamp and a Chinese trusted timestamp, and anyone can verify an exported certificate without Bernstein.

Price: Pricing varies (Bernstein pricing). Suede AI vs Bernstein

Alternative 5

5. Numbers Protocol

Numbers Protocol builds provenance infrastructure on its own Numbers blockchain. Its flagship ProofSnap camera app for iOS writes GPS, timestamp and device data to a blockchain the moment you press the shutter and attaches a C2PA manifest to each photo or video, with no wallet or seed phrase. A ProofSnap browser extension captures full web pages with C2PA credentials, and the stack builds on open standards including C2PA 2.x and EIP-7053.

Price: Pricing varies (ProofSnap on the App Store). Suede AI vs Numbers Protocol
