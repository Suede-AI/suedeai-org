07 / Research

Research: creator ownership and provenance.

Secondary to the platform work. These pages hold Jason Colapietro’s research on what happens to authorship, consent and payment when AI makes media cheap to produce.

The research sits beside Suede AI’s music and creator software, a separate product line. Each page treats one piece on its own terms and states its limits: a proof-of-creation record is evidence, not a registration; provenance documents history, not every legal right; and a registry record does not establish consent, prevent copying, or guarantee payment.

How do I invest in Suede AI?

Suede AI is raising a pre-seed on a SAFE, its first equity round. Request the investor brief or book an intro call.

What does "proof of creation" actually mean?

A proof-of-creation record stores a file fingerprint, a wallet-signed creator claim, contributor metadata, and a timestamp. The record is public evidence to review; it does not independently establish authorship or legal ownership, issue or verify licenses, or route royalties. See proof of creation.
