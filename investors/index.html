01 The thesis

Creation got cheap. Ownership didn't scale.

Cost to createAI era

Heading toward zero.

AI lowered the cost of making songs, images, video, campaigns, lessons, and synthetic media to near zero. Value is moving away from production.

vs

What creators and agents needScarce

Proof that travels.

Authorship, voice, likeness, consent, provenance, rights, payment, and royalties: the layer that turns work into durable value.

That shifts value toward the layer creators, agents, platforms, and fans need to turn work into durable value. The investor question is not whether another tool can generate media.

The question is who owns the rails when creative work has to be proven, licensed, distributed, and paid out across humans and AI agents.

02 The model

Four stages. One ownership spine.

  1. 01

    Create

    AI lowers the cost of making media of every kind.

  2. 02

    Prove

    Authorship, voice, likeness, consent, provenance, and rights travel with the work.

    Suede's wedge
  3. 03

    Launch

    Projects get routes to funding, audience, and ownership participation.

  4. 04

    Earn

    Payments, vaults, royalties, licensing, distribution, and agent commerce turn output into income.

Everything downstream settles against the proof layer. That layer is where Suede starts.

Honest boundary
